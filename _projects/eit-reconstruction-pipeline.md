An engineering pipeline for AI-enhanced electrical impedance tomography
(EIT), combining:

- **Boundary simulation** under 30 dB Gaussian noise to mimic realistic sensor conditions
- **Jacobian-based linearized difference reconstruction** for the forward/inverse problem
- **A convolutional neural network** for structural image refinement and hard-mask semantic parsing

Applied to synthetic phantom slices modeling the human brain, the pipeline
was evaluated for feasibility in detecting neurocysticercosis (NCC)-like
anomalies, reaching an ROC AUC of 0.861.
