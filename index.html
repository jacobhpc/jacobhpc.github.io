About

My recent work centers on the intersection of inverse problems, deep learning, and biomedical signal processing. Recent research applies Jacobian-based linearized difference reconstruction alongside convolutional neural networks to boost diagnostic feasibility of EIT for detecting brain anomalies (including a preliminary study on neurocysticercosis (NCC) detection under simulated low-resource constraints).

I'm interested in theoretical and medical physics. Primarily working on medical and nuclear physics.
